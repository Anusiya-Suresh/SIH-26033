## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.



1. Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is FarmLink, an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers directly.

The platform allows farmers to list their products, set prices, and receive orders without depending on multiple intermediaries. Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices. The platform also provides logistics support, AI-based demand forecasting, and optimized delivery routes.

Key features:
- Direct connection between farmers and buyers.
- Transparent pricing and product details.
- AI-based demand forecasting to predict product demand.
- Smart logistics and route optimization.
- Order tracking and secure digital payments.
- Bulk ordering facilities for retailers and businesses.
The platform aims to improve farmers' earnings, reduce consumer costs, and make agricultural distribution more efficient.


2. Technical Approach
The project uses modern web technologies, artificial intelligence, and location-based services.

Technologies to be used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap.
- Backend: Python and Django.
- Database: MySQL.
- AI/ML: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn.
- Maps and routing: Google Maps Platform or OpenStreetMap with a routing service.
- Payments: A suitable payment gateway.
- Deployment: Cloud hosting.

  
Implementation methodology:
Farmer Registration and Product Listing

Digital Marketplace and Price Comparison

AI Demand Forecasting and Order Management

Logistics and Route Optimization

Delivery, Payment, and Feedback


Development process:
1. Collect requirements from farmers and buyers.
2. Develop registration, product listing, and ordering modules.
3. Integrate demand forecasting and route optimization.
4. Test the platform with sample agricultural data.
5. Deploy a prototype and collect user feedback.

   
3. Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses widely available technologies and can initially be developed as a web application.

Feasibility:
- A basic prototype can be developed using open-source technologies.
- Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
- AI features can be introduced gradually as sufficient data becomes available.

Potential challenges:
- Limited digital literacy among some farmers.
- Inconsistent internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Product quality, freshness, and availability.
- Transportation costs and delivery delays.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.
  
Strategies to overcome challenges:
- Provide a simple, multilingual interface.
- Support assistance through FPOs and local coordinators.
- Verify product listings and maintain transparent prices.
- Partner with local transport providers and warehouses.
- Introduce buyer reviews, order tracking, and clear dispute-resolution procedures.
  
Viability: The platform can generate revenue through a small transaction fee, logistics service charges, and optional services for bulk buyers. Keeping fees transparent and affordable can support long-term sustainability.


4. Impact and Benefits

Benefits for Farmers
- Better price realization by reducing intermediaries.
- Direct access to more customers.
- Improved planning through demand predictions.
- Reduced risk of unsold produce.




Benefits for Consumers
- Potentially lower prices.
- Access to fresh agricultural products.
- Transparent pricing and product information.
- Convenient online ordering.




Economic and Environmental Benefits
- Improved supply-chain efficiency.
- Better utilization of transport.
- Potential reduction in food wastage.
- Support for rural employment and local businesses.



Overall impact: FarmLink aims to create a fairer agricultural supply chain by improving market access for farmers and making agricultural products more accessible to consumers.
